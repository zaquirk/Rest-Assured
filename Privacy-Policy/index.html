Rest Assured Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Rest Assured respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information Stored

Rest Assured may store:

  • Instrument and practice-session information
  • Practice notes, goals, reflections, categories, and dates
  • Practice routines and preferences
  • Audio recordings created using the app

Most information is stored locally on your device. Practice data may also be synchronized through Apple’s private iCloud CloudKit service when iCloud is enabled. This data is associated with your Apple account and is not shared publicly with other Rest Assured users.

Audio recordings and app preferences remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Information We Collect

Rest Assured does not collect personal information, require a separate account, use advertising, or use third-party analytics or tracking services.

Rest Assured does not access or sell information stored in your private iCloud database.

Data Sharing

Rest Assured does not sell, rent, or share your personal information. Data may be processed by Apple through iCloud and CloudKit to provide synchronization and storage services.

Data Export and Deletion

You can export your practice history using the export tools within the app. You can delete your practice data from the app or remove the app from your device. iCloud-stored data may also be managed through your Apple iCloud settings.

Children’s Privacy

Rest Assured does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL.
